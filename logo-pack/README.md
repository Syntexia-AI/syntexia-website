# Syntexia Logo Pack

The Syntexia mark is a 5×5 lattice of dots — an emergent "S" with a copper
accent at centre. It animates on the website ("Lattice Emerge"), but the static
versions here work everywhere else.

## Files

### Master (vector — preferred for anything)
- `syntexia-mark.svg`         — transparent background
- `syntexia-mark-dark.svg`    — on near-black surface (#0B0A08)
- `syntexia-mark-light.svg`   — on warm paper surface (#F6F2EA)
- `syntexia-wordmark.svg`     — horizontal lockup: mark + "Syntexia.AI"
- `og-image.svg`               — social link preview (1200×630)

### PNG renders (for tools that don't accept SVG)
- `syntexia-mark-64.png` through `-1024.png`         — transparent
- `syntexia-mark-dark-64.png` through `-1024.png`    — on dark
- `syntexia-mark-light-64.png` through `-1024.png`   — on light
- `favicon-32.png`, `favicon-48.png`                — browser tab icon

## Colours

- Ink (light surface): `#1A1814`
- Ink (dark surface):  `#F4F0E6`
- Copper accent:       `#D9A56B` (light: `#C18341`)
- Mute / off dots:     `#79705E` at 32% opacity

## Clear space

Leave at least 1 dot-width of empty space on every side of the mark.

## What not to do

- Don't rotate the mark.
- Don't recolour the accent dot to anything other than copper.
- Don't stretch — keep the lattice square.
- Don't substitute the wordmark font.
